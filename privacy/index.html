Privacy

KARE · EFFECTIVE 18 OCTOBER 2026

Your thoughts stay on your phone. Once a thought has passed, its words are gone.

What Kare keeps

Kare has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don't run any servers, and nothing you write is ever sent to us or anyone else.

Everything the app keeps is stored in its own folder on your phone:

  • the thought you are letting go, until it has passed. Then its words are deleted, and the app keeps only when it passed and how many have passed in that tray;
  • the name you'd like to be greeted by, any names you give your sand pits, and a picture of each pit's sand so it looks as you left it;
  • a few settings, such as sound, your sand and tray, and whether you'd like a notice.

Smoothing the sand by hand also deletes the thought's words. If you back up your phone to iCloud or a computer, this folder is included in that backup, the same as any other app's data, and is protected by your backup's own settings.

Kare's widgets and its Live Activity read the same folder to show your sand on your Home Screen and Lock Screen. That stays on your phone too.

Motion and sound

Kare reads your phone's motion sensors so the sand tray drifts a little as you hold it. These readings are used in the moment and are never stored or sent. Kare plays sound but never uses the microphone.

Notifications

Kare can send a quiet notice when a thought has passed while you were away, and, if you turn it on, one reminder a day. It asks first, and only if you say yes. The notice is scheduled on your phone itself. No push service is used, and nothing about it leaves your device. You can turn it off any time in your phone's Settings.

Kare Premium

Kare Premium is an optional one-time purchase. It is sold and paid for through Apple, and Kare never sees your payment details or Apple ID. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

To know whether you have Kare Premium, Kare uses RevenueCat, a purchase service. When the app opens, and when you look at Kare Premium, buy or restore, it contacts RevenueCat with:

  • a random identifier the app makes up. It isn't linked to your name, email or Apple ID;
  • your App Store purchase history for Kare;
  • basic technical details that come with any such request, such as the app version, your phone's system version, its App Store country and its IP address.

RevenueCat uses this only to work out whether you have Kare Premium, under the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. None of your thoughts, tray names or settings are ever sent.

Updates

Kare can receive small fixes without a new App Store download. When the app opens, it asks Expo's update service, EAS Update, whether one is ready. That request carries the app's version, your phone's system, a random identifier for this install of Kare and, as with any request, your IP address. It is used only to send the right update, under the Expo Privacy Policy. None of your thoughts, names or settings are sent.

Links

If you choose to look for a helpline from inside the app, Kare opens findahelpline.com in your browser, and that site's own privacy policy applies there.

Children

Kare isn't directed at children under 13, and Kare itself collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Deleting your data

Deleting Kare from your phone deletes everything it kept. Any backups you have made keep their copy until you delete those backups.

Kare Premium belongs to your Apple Account, so deleting the app doesn't lose it: choose Restore in Kare's settings to bring it back. To ask RevenueCat to delete the purchase records tied to your random identifier, write to us and we'll pass the request on.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.

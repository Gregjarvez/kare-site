Kare Premium

Kare Premium is one purchase, yours for good. It isn't a subscription, so there is nothing to cancel and nothing renews.

On a new phone, or after reinstalling, open Kare's settings and choose Restore to bring it back. It is tied to your Apple Account, not to this phone.

Refunds are handled by Apple. You can ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your thoughts

What you write stays on your phone, so we can't see it, recover it or remove it for you. Once a thought has passed, its words are gone. The privacy policy says exactly what Kare keeps.

If it's more than that

Kare is a quiet ritual, not a treatment. If a thought is more than it can carry, findahelpline.com lists free, confidential helplines in your country.
